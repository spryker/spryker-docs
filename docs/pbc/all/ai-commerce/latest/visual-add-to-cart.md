Visual Add to Cart lets buyers upload a product image, a screenshot, or a PDF order form on the Quick Order page. AI recognizes the ordered products and quantities and pre-fills the quick order form with matching SKUs.

**Use case:** A buyer photographs a shelf, a product list, or product packaging, or exports a purchase order as a PDF from their procurement system. The system identifies the products and quantities in the file, finds matching SKUs in the catalog, and pre-fills the quick order form — enabling rapid bulk ordering without manual entry.

Accepted file formats: JPEG, PNG, and PDF (up to 10 MB). A PDF can be digital or scanned and can have several pages.

## Use Visual Add to Cart on the Storefront

1. Open the Quick Order page.
2. Click the upload button.
3. Upload a photo or a PDF order form where one or more products are recognizable.
   The recognized products are pre-filled in the quick order form.
4. Review the pre-filled items and adjust if needed.
5. Add the items to cart.

## Enable Visual Add to Cart

To enable the feature, in the Back Office, go to **AI Commerce&nbsp;<span aria-label="and then">></span>&nbsp;Quick Order&nbsp;<span aria-label="and then">></span>&nbsp;Visual Add to Cart** and turn it on.
